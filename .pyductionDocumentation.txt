This outlines the CURRENT version of the Pyduction Solver.
###################################################################################################################################
    ~~ HOW IT WORKS ~~

LETTER PERCENTAGE
    - Calculated for each guess based on the list of possible words.

INFO SCORE
    - Adds together each letter in a word's percentage, but skips duplicate letters and greys.
        The skip means:
            - words spamming high value letters aren't overrepresented.
            - greys add nothing to the word score as they neither gain info nor can be a correct guess... 
                though sometimes they'll be needed to guess a word with high info, so we can't cull words with greys.
PROB SCORE 
    - Identical as info score, but any impossible guesses have a score of 0.
    - Only considered on the 6th guess. 

GUESS
    - The guess is always the word with maximum score.
    - If there is a tie for maximum score it randomly picks between the maximums.
